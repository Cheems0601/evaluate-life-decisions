# China Source Module

Use this module for decisions materially affected by conditions in mainland China. Source names remain in Chinese when that helps precise retrieval. This is a search route, not an ingested book library.

## Current conditions

| Topic | Starting points | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Economy, population, employment, cities | 国家统计局 and its 国家数据 portal; relevant provincial or municipal statistical bureaus; education and human resources authorities | data year, national versus local scope, total versus field or occupation subgroup |
| Digital economy and AI | 国家数据局 reports; the original issuing authority for policies; scientific papers, technical documentation, and original job postings | policy target versus observed adoption; report date versus data period |
| Education and research | 教育部; official university and graduate program pages; 国家自然科学基金委员会 where relevant | degree and field, domestic/international applicant rules, cohort date |
| Industry and occupations | original employer postings; sector regulators; industry associations with disclosed methods; local public employment services | city, seniority, compensation basis, sample selection, publication date |

For international comparison, use the global sources in `regional-sources.md` and reconcile definition differences.

## Historical and structural research leads

| Period | Sources or research leads | Useful mechanisms | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Early China to Han | *Shiji* (《史记》) | critical choices, alliances, advancement and retreat | verify chapter and edition; do not invent interior motives |
| Warring States to Five Dynasties | *Zizhi Tongjian* (《资治通鉴》) | institutions, timing, coalitions, long run costs | compiled perspective; does not cover the Song and later periods |
| Ming | *Ming Shi* (《明史》), accessible *Ming Shilu* records, and checked modern scholarship | individual action under institutional constraints | separate records from later interpretation |
| Late Qing transformation | specialist scholarship such as Chen Xulu and Mao Haijian, checked in the relevant edition | external shock, information failure, institutional adaptation | background does not directly predict a modern career outcome |
| Republican era and resistance war | archives and peer reviewed or specialist histories, with disputed claims cross checked | severe uncertainty, resource limits, institutional change | do not casually map wartime choices onto ordinary personal decisions |
| 1949 onward | official statistics, economic histories, reform era scholarship, and firm level records | industrialization, urbanization, education expansion, market and technology shifts | distinguish stages and changing institutions; verify current conditions separately |

Modern books may be listed as retrieval leads, but the project does not bundle their text. Popular narrative histories may help discover a possible event; verify the event and interpretation through primary records or specialist research before using it.

## China specific transfer checks

- Is the choice affected by hukou, public sector recruitment, local industrial clusters, graduate education policy, or regional public services?
- Does a national statistic hide large city, province, institution, or field differences?
- Is a stated policy direction supported by implementation, funding, hiring, or observed adoption?
- Has the relevant institutional stage changed enough to invalidate an older case?
